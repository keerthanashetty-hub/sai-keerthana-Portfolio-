Project — 01
Zomato vs Swiggy Hyperlocal Demand Analysis
Food Tech
🎯 Problem Statement

With Bengaluru's food delivery market split between Zomato and Swiggy, restaurants lack clarity on platform-specific demand, cuisine trends, and price sensitivity by locality. This project curated and analyzed 2,000+ real-world restaurant records from both platforms to uncover hyperlocal patterns and enable data-backed decisions on platform selection, pricing strategy, and cuisine positioning.

🗂️ Dataset

2,000+ restaurant records across Bengaluru from both platforms. Applied null-value treatment, deduplication and type normalization.

📄 zomato_very_clean.csv 📄 swiggy_new_clean.csv
⚙️ Process
  • Merged Zomato & Swiggy CSVs; cleaned nulls, duplicates & cuisine labels using Pandas
  • EDA: locality-wise density, cuisine frequency, cost distribution, rating patterns
  • Cross-platform KPI comparison: cost category, ratings, total restaurants per zone
  • Built interactive Power BI dashboard with DAX measures, slicers & drill-downs
💡 Insights & Conclusion
  • North Indian & Chinese cuisines dominated 80%+ localities on both platforms
  • Swiggy led outer Bengaluru; Zomato dominated central and premium zones
  • Mid-range pricing ₹200 to ₹500 showed highest ratings correlation across areas
  • Identified 6 high-demand, low-supply micro-zones as strategic gaps for new entries

Project — 02
Dynamic Pricing Strategy for Fitness Classes at Cult.fit
Fitness
🎯 Problem Statement

Cult.fit faced a dual challenge: peak slots were overbooked while off-peak hours remained severely underutilized, resulting in consistent revenue leakage. This project analyzed 3,000+ fitness class records spanning April to June 2018 across multiple cities, covering diverse class types (Yoga, HIIT, Cycling, Zumba) and booking time windows. The objective was to uncover attendance patterns, identify demand-supply gaps, and build a data-driven dynamic pricing framework with tiered pricing to optimize class occupancy and maximize overall revenue performance.

⚙️ Process
  • EDA on attendance by hour, day, class type & location using Pandas & Seaborn
  • Identified peak vs off-peak windows via time-series & statistical analysis
  • Segmented demand into High / Medium / Low using percentile-based classification
  • Applied regression modeling to forecast expected attendance per slot & type
  • Designed a 3-tier dynamic pricing matrix mapped to demand segments
💡 Insights & Conclusion
  • Morning slots (6 to 9 AM) and HIIT classes showed 40%+ higher attendance
  • Weekend evenings had highest booking dropout, confirming pricing sensitivity
  • Off-peak Yoga & Zumba had 35% unfilled capacity — prime for discount tiers
  • Tiered pricing (₹299 / ₹499 / ₹699) projected to improve occupancy by ~22%
  • Recommendations delivered to reduce revenue leakage across all city locations
